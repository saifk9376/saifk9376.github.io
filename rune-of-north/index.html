Need help, or found a bug?
Email saifk9376@gmail.com. Every message gets read. It helps to say which phone you play on.

My progress is gone after reinstalling

On iPhone your progress is kept in your iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple account and open the game once with an internet connection, and it comes back.

How do I start over?

In the game, open Settings and tap Reset all progress. This erases your best height, runes, heroes and Phoenix Blood on the phone and in iCloud. Your settings stay.

When do ads show?

Only when you choose to watch one: after a run, to double the runes you earned, or, when you have no Phoenix Blood left, to rise again once per run. Nothing interrupts a climb.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Europe and the UK, open Settings in the game and tap Your privacy choices. On iPhone you can also change tracking in your phone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

How do I report an ad?

If an ad seemed wrong for the game or for young players, tap the small ⓘ on the ad itself to report it to Google straight away. You can also email saifk9376@gmail.com with roughly when you saw it and what it showed, and we will block it.
